# Gossip Harbor – Cheat Sheet (iOS)

A quick-reference guide for **Gossip Harbor: Merge & Story** (Microfun) on iPhone/iPad.
Legit strategy only – no hacks, mod APKs or "free gems" generators (they get accounts banned and are usually scams).

> Exact numbers (energy cap, timers, prices) shift between game updates and events. Treat figures here as rules of thumb.

---

## 1. Core loop in 30 seconds

1. **Tap producers** (generators) → spend energy → spawn level-1 items.
2. **Drag 2 identical items together** → merge into the next level.
3. **Fill orders** from customers at the top of the board → earn coins + XP (stars).
4. **Spend stars/coins** on renovation tasks → unlock story chapters and new areas.
5. Repeat. Energy is the bottleneck – everything below is about stretching it.

---

## 2. Energy – the #1 resource

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Spend energy before you hit the cap – full bar = wasted regen | Let the bar sit full overnight |
| Claim free energy from daily tasks, the mailbox, level-ups and event rewards | Buy energy with gems early on |
| Check the in-game shop for free/ad energy bundles each day | Tap producers "just because" – only tap for items you need |
| Save energy for events with double rewards | Use energy boosters (x2/x4) before you understand the chains |

**Level-ups usually give energy.** If you're about to level up, burn your remaining energy first so the reward doesn't push you over the cap.

---

## 3. Board management

- **Keep the board ~⅓ empty.** A full board blocks new spawns and bubble rewards.
- **Sell junk:** tap an item → sell for coins if it belongs to a chain no current order needs.
- **Inventory/storage:** park high-level items and future-order pieces there. Expanding slots is one of the best gem purchases.
- **Stack by chain:** group items from the same producer in one area so pairs are easy to spot.
- **Don't merge blindly.** Orders often ask for a *specific* level – merging past it wastes the item.

---

## 4. Orders

- Prioritise orders that **complete a story task** or give the **most stars**.
- Look at *all* open orders before merging – one item may serve two customers.
- Orders with a clock/special icon are often event or bonus orders – do those first.
- If an order needs a chain you don't have yet, ignore it; it will wait.

---

## 5. Producers (generators)

- Producers themselves can be **merged/upgraded** → higher-level producers spawn better items and more per charge.
- When a producer is **recharging**, don't spend gems to skip unless an event is ending.
- Some producers are consumable (run out after N taps) – use them for orders, don't waste them on random taps.
- Chests/boxes that drop from merges contain free items – open them when you have board space.

---

## 6. Bubbles

- Merging high-level items sometimes spawns a **bubble** with a bonus copy.
- Popping it costs gems; if you wait, it turns into a few coins.
- **Only pop** if the item is high-level, needed for a current order, or would take lots of energy to rebuild. Otherwise let it expire.

---

## 7. Gems – spend wisely

**Good uses**
1. Extra inventory slots
2. Popping a bubble with a very high-level item
3. Finishing an event milestone right before it ends

**Bad uses**
- Buying energy early
- Skipping short producer timers
- Speeding up anything that finishes while you sleep

Free gems: achievements, level-ups, daily tasks, event milestone rewards, mailbox gifts.

---

## 8. Events

- Events (races, collection/dig events, seasonal chapters) give **far better rewards per energy** than the main board.
- Read the event rules once – many use their own board or tokens.
- **Race events:** join early in the day; spend saved energy in one burst.
- Hoard energy/items a little before a new event starts if you can see it coming.
- Collect milestone rewards *before* the timer ends – unclaimed rewards may be lost.

---

## 9. Daily routine (5 minutes)

- [ ] Collect mailbox / daily gifts
- [ ] Claim free shop items
- [ ] Spend energy down (don't leave it capped)
- [ ] Complete daily tasks
- [ ] Check active event progress & timers
- [ ] Clear junk from board, reorganise inventory

---

## 10. Account & settings

- **Link your account** (Game Center / Facebook / in-game ID) so progress survives a new phone.
- Turn on notifications for **"energy full"** so you never waste regen.
- Progress is cloud-saved; iCloud device backups alone won't restore it without a linked account.
- Contact in-game support (Settings → Help) for lost purchases – include your player ID.

---

## Quick glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Producer / generator | Tappable item that spawns a chain's base items |
| Chain | All levels of one item family |
| Order | Customer request at the top of the board |
| Stars | XP earned from orders, spent on story/renovation tasks |
| Bubble | Temporary bonus item – pay gems to keep |
| Inventory | Off-board storage slots |
