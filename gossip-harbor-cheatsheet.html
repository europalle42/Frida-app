Harbor cheat sheet

Strategy tips for getting further on less energy. Only fair play: no hacks, modded apps or "free gems" generators. Those are scams, or they get your account banned.

Energy caps, timers and prices change between game updates and events, so treat the numbers here as rules of thumb.

Core loop in 30 seconds

  1. Tap producers to spend energy and spawn level-1 items.
  2. Drag two identical items together to merge them into the next level.
  3. Fill orders from customers at the top of the board for coins and XP.
  4. Spend your rewards on renovation tasks to unlock story chapters and new areas.

Energy is the limit on everything. Most of the tips below help you get more out of it.

Energy

Do

  • Spend energy before it reaches the cap. A full bar stops refilling.
  • Claim free energy from daily tasks, the mailbox, level-ups and events.
  • Check the shop every day for free or ad-based energy.
  • Save energy for events with double rewards.

Don't

  • Leave the bar full overnight.
  • Buy energy with gems early in the game.
  • Tap producers for items no order needs.
  • Use x2/x4 energy boosters before you know the item chains.

Level-ups usually give energy. When you're close to a level-up, spend your energy first so the reward doesn't push you over the cap.

Board management

  • Keep about a third of the board empty. A full board blocks new spawns and bonus items.
  • Sell junk. Tap an item and sell it for coins if no current order needs its chain.
  • Use storage. Park high-level items and pieces for future orders there. Extra slots are one of the best gem buys.
  • Group by chain. Keep items from the same producer together so pairs are easy to spot.
  • Don't merge blindly. Orders often ask for a specific level, and merging past it wastes the item.

Orders

  • Do the orders that finish a story task or pay the most first.
  • Read all open orders before merging. One item might cover two customers.
  • Orders with a clock or special icon are often event or bonus orders. Do those first.
  • If an order needs a chain you don't have yet, leave it. It will wait.

Producers

  • Merge producers to upgrade them. Higher levels spawn better items and more per charge.
  • Don't pay gems to skip a recharge unless an event is about to end.
  • Some producers run out after a set number of taps. Use those taps for orders only.
  • Open chests and boxes from merges when you have room on the board.

Bubbles

  • Merging high-level items sometimes spawns a bubble holding a bonus copy.
  • Popping it costs gems. If you wait, it turns into a few coins.
  • Pop it only if the item is high-level, needed for an order, or would cost a lot of energy to rebuild.

Gems

Worth it

  • Extra storage slots
  • A bubble with a very high-level item
  • Finishing an event milestone just before it ends

Waste

  • Buying energy early on
  • Skipping short producer timers
  • Speeding up anything that finishes while you sleep

Free gems come from achievements, level-ups, daily tasks, event milestones and mailbox gifts.

Events

  • Events (races, collection or dig events, seasonal chapters) pay far more per energy than the main board.
  • Read the rules once. Many events use their own board or tokens.
  • Races: join early in the day and spend saved energy in one burst.
  • If you can see a new event coming, save some energy and items for it.
  • Claim milestone rewards before the timer ends. Unclaimed rewards can be lost.

Account and settings

  • Link your account (Game Center, Facebook or in-game ID) so your progress moves to a new phone.
  • Turn on the "energy full" notification so you never waste a refill.
  • An iCloud backup alone won't restore your game without a linked account.
  • For lost purchases, contact support in the game's settings and include your player ID.
